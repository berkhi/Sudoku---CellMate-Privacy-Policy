SUDOKU - CELLMATE Privacy Policy Last Updated: May 2, 2026 Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. It explains how Sudoku - CellMate ("App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. 1. Overview & Key Principles Sudoku - CellMate is designed with privacy as a priority: We do NOT collect any personal information directly from you. All game data (progress, scores, settings) is stored locally on your device only. No data is transmitted to our servers — we do not operate any backend servers. The only third-party service integrated is Google AdMob for advertising, which may collect limited data as described below. 2. Information We Collect 2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect We do not collect, store, or process any of the following: Your name, email address, or any contact information Account credentials or passwords Payment or financial information Precise GPS location data Photos, contacts, or files from your device Any personally identifiable information (PII) 2.2 Information Collected by Google AdMob (Third Party) The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob ("AdMob"), a service of Google LLC. AdMob may automatically collect certain information from your device to serve ads, including: Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS / GAID on Android) Device type, model, and operating system version General location (country/region level, derived from IP address) IP address App interaction data (ad impressions, clicks) Language and time zone settings This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google AdMob's data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy 2.3 Local Device Storage The App stores the following data locally on your device using an on-device database (local storage only, never transmitted externally): Game progress and completed puzzle history Personal best scores and statistics App settings and preferences This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. 3. How Information Is Used We do not use any personal data because we do not collect any. The only use of data is by Google AdMob for the following purposes: Displaying advertisements within the App Serving personalized or contextual ads based on your device's advertising profile Fraud detection and prevention Analytics and reporting for advertisers 4. Advertising (Google AdMob) 4.1 Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads By default, AdMob may serve personalized advertisements based on your interests and device data. Depending on your region and applicable laws (e.g., GDPR in Europe), you may be presented with a consent prompt when you first launch the App. If you decline or opt out of personalized ads, AdMob will serve contextual (non-personalized) ads instead. 4.2 Opting Out of Personalized Advertising You can control ad personalization at the device level: For iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track" or revoke permission for this app Alternatively: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising > Disable "Personalized Ads" For Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads Tap "Delete advertising ID" or enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" Note: Opting out of personalization does not remove ads from the App — you will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to your interests. 4.3 Remove Ads (Future Feature) We may offer a one-time in-app purchase to remove advertisements in the future. If implemented: The purchase will be processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, each with their own privacy and payment policies. We will not directly collect or store your payment information. A local flag on your device will store your purchase status to disable ads. This policy will be updated before any such purchase feature is activated. 5. Sharing of Information We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for their own purposes. The only data sharing that occurs is through Google AdMob's standard operation as described in Section 4. We have no control over the data practices of Google AdMob beyond what is outlined in our agreement with them. 6. Data Retention Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our end. Local game data is retained on your device until you uninstall the App or clear the App's data through your device settings. AdMob-collected data is retained according to Google's data retention policies. 7.
