Overview & Key Principles

Sudoku – CellMate is designed with privacy as a priority. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices.

✅ We do not collect personal information directly.  ·  ✅ All game data is stored locally on your device.  ·  ✅ No data is transmitted to our servers — we do not operate any backend servers.

Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any of the following:

  • Precise GPS location data

The App displays advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect:

The App stores the following data locally on your device only — it is never transmitted externally:

Advertising (Google AdMob)

4.1 Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads

By default, AdMob may serve personalized advertisements based on your interests and device data. Depending on your region and applicable laws (e.g., GDPR in Europe), you may be presented with a consent prompt on first launch. If you decline, AdMob will serve contextual (non-personalized) ads instead.

4.2 Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You can control ad personalization at the device level:

🍎 iOS
  1. Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  2. Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  3. Or: Settings → Privacy → Apple Advertising → Disable "Personalized Ads"
🤖 Android
  1. Settings → Google → Ads
  2. Tap "Delete advertising ID"
  3. Or enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

⚠️ Opting out of personalization does not remove ads from the App — you will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to your interests.

4.3 Remove Ads (Future Feature)

We may offer a one-time in-app purchase to remove advertisements in the future. If implemented:

  • The purchase will be processed through Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
  • We will not directly collect or store your payment information.
  • A local flag on your device will store your purchase status to disable ads.
  • This policy will be updated before any such feature is activated.
